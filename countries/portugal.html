Lisbon

We spent only 4 days in Lisbon, of which one was dedicated to a day trip to Sintra.
We went in May, so it was not too hot. The city is very beautiful and has a lot of history, mixing Moorish and Roman architecture.
Sintra in particular is a must-see, with its beautiful palaces and gardens. In detail, we visited Quinta da Regaleira, the Pena Palace, and the Monserrate Palace.
Although Lisbon is really lovely, Sintra was definitely the highlight of our trip.

Where to Eat in Lisbon

Lisbon has quite a lot of options for gluten-free and vegetarian food. If vegetarian is not a restriction, then you must try the local seafood.

  • Coppola Pizzeria. Hands down in the top 5 pizzas we have ever eaten. The pizza dough is very tasty and the toppings are excellent.
    I know it sounds like heresy since Portuguese food is amazing - especially the seafood - and it would have deserved more exploration, but this pizza was so good that we came here 3 times in 4 days.

    Ratings:
    • 5/5: excellent pizza with tasty toppings.
    • 5/5: the vegetarian selection is also great.
  • Altar. This restaurant close to Alfama and the São Jorge castle is a great place to have a nice traditional meal. The food is very good and the service is excellent.
    We had a fish fillet with potatoes and stuffed bell peppers. I must say, I preferred the vegetarian option over the fish.

    Ratings:
    • 4/5: excellent gluten-free options.
    • 4/5: great vegetarian alternatives.
